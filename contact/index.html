Common Questions

FAQ

الأسئلة الشائعة

How long does a typical facade project take?

A complete facade design and installation project typically takes 8–16 weeks from contract signing to completion, depending on complexity. Facade transformation projects can be completed in 4–8 weeks.

Do you work outside Saudi Arabia?

Yes. OSOOL has completed projects in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and Egypt. We manage logistics and installation across the entire Gulf region and North Africa.

Can I see examples of GRC elements before committing?

Absolutely. We maintain a physical sample library at our Riyadh studio, and we provide AI-rendered visualizations of all elements in context on your specific facade before any fabrication begins.

What is the minimum project size you work with?

We work on projects ranging from single entrance gate designs to complete estate transformations. Our minimum engagement starts from SAR 150,000 for design and specification services.

Do you provide a warranty on GRC installation?

Yes. All OSOOL GRC installations carry a 5-year structural warranty and a 10-year material warranty against colour fade and surface degradation under normal exterior conditions.

How does the AI visualization process work?

After an initial consultation, our team generates multiple facade concept variations using generative AI tools, refined by our master architects. You receive photorealistic renders and 360° walkthroughs before approving any direction for fabrication.
